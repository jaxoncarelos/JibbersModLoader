## `env.manager`

Scene-wide environment controller: runs every frame in a map, owns the post-processing (Beautify) volume and receives the map-load event.

Update is the per-frame hook Atmosphere, Reshred and FastLoad share (3 plugins: the per-method limit is reached). The map-load handler may live on terrain.painter instead on some builds.
